# Contributor covenant

Orchestrarr is a community project. Participants are expected to be respectful,
direct and constructive. Discuss code, documentation and decisions without
attacking people. Do not publish private information, credentials, abusive
content or material that puts another person at risk.

Maintainers may remove comments, close issues or reject contributions that
violate these expectations. For conduct concerns, contact the repository
maintainer privately through the contact method listed on the project site.
