# Orchestrarr name and marks

Orchestrarr, the Orchestrarr wordmark and the Orchestrarr logo identify the
official project maintained at <https://github.com/xSpylon/orchestrarr>
and presented at <https://orchestrarr.com>.

The source code is available under the [GNU Affero General Public License,
version 3](LICENSE). That license grants rights to use, modify, distribute and
sell the code; it does not grant permission to use the Orchestrarr name, logo
or other project identifiers in a way that suggests official status or
endorsement.

## Forks and redistributions

You may accurately say that a product is based on Orchestrarr or compatible
with Orchestrarr. A modified distribution should use a different product name,
logo and visual identity unless the maintainers have given written permission
to use the official marks.

Please keep the original copyright, license and attribution notices required
by the AGPL-3.0 license. Do not remove the link to the official project or
claim that a fork is an official Orchestrarr release.

## Commercial services

Commercial hosting, support and integrations are welcome under the software
license. Use of the official marks must still make the relationship clear. For
example, “managed hosting for Orchestrarr” is descriptive; “Orchestrarr Cloud”
requires permission from the project maintainers.

This policy is intended to prevent confusion about project identity. It does
not itself establish or replace a trademark registration.
